Dr. Ahmad Ali is Professor in the Department of Islamic Studies at the University of Chittagong. He joined the University of Chittagong faculty in 1996 and has served as Professor since 2008.

He earned his PhD in Arabic Literature from the University of Chittagong, after completing BA (Honours) and MA degrees in Arabic with first-class results. He later completed a postgraduate diploma in training Arabic teachers for non-Arabic speakers at King Saud University in Riyadh.
