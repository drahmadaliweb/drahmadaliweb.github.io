Academic Profile

Scholar, educator and academic leader.

A concise view of Dr. Ahmad Ali’s academic formation, scholarly identity, research interests, current intellectual projects and institutional contribution. For the chronological life story, see the dedicated biography.

Dr. Ahmad Ali is a Professor in the Department of Islamic Studies at the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh. His academic and scholarly work spans Islamic studies, Arabic language and literature, fiqh, Islamic law, theology, Tasawwuf, Hadith, Sirah, governance and contemporary Muslim thought.

He began his professional career in Islamic education before joining the University of Chittagong as a lecturer in 1996. Over the following decades he progressed through the academic ranks and has served as Professor since 2008. He also served as Chairman of the Department of Islamic Studies from 2015 to 2016.

His scholarly output includes research books, Bengali and Arabic research articles, edited publications and translation work. He has also supervised doctoral and MPhil research and has been associated with academic, legal-research and Shariah-related institutions in Bangladesh.

Research Profile

Research grounded in Islamic intellectual traditions.

His scholarship brings classical Islamic intellectual traditions into sustained engagement with questions of law, belief, society, language and contemporary thought.

01

Islamic Jurisprudence & Law

Fiqh, rights, governance and legal reasoning.

Research themes include comparative fiqh, juristic disagreement, prioritization in Islamic law, Islamic criminal law, the rights and status of non-Muslim citizens, housing rights and security, governance, sovereignty, Shariah compliance in banking and the relationship between Islamic and prevailing legal systems.

FiqhIslamic LawRightsGovernanceShariah Banking
02

Arabic Language & Literature

Literature, grammar, writing and intellectual history.

His Arabic scholarship includes modern Arabic poetry, Arabic literary movements, language acquisition, Arabic grammar and grammarians, the origins and development of Arabic writing, Arabic and Persian calligraphic traditions, and the influence of Western literature on modern Arabic writing.

Arabic LiteraturePoetryGrammarCalligraphyLanguage
03

Islamic Thought & Theology

Aqidah, spirituality and contemporary ideologies.

His books and articles address the foundations of faith, kufr, shirk and nifaq, Tasawwuf, tazkiyat al-nafs, bid‘ah, the qualities of religious scholars, modern intellectual movements and ideologies, and questions concerning Islam, democracy and political thought.

AqidahTasawwufTazkiyahModern ThoughtReligious Scholarship
04

Hadith & Prophetic Studies

Hadith, Sirah, the Companions and Islamic ethics.

His work includes studies of Hadith as a source of Shariah, the writing and transmission of Hadith, Sirah and the Madinan period, the consultative practice of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, the governance of Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (ra), the Companions, prophetic ethics and religious education.

HadithSirahSahabahShuraIslamic Ethics
