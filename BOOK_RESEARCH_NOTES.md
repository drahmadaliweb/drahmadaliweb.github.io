# Book research notes — remaining catalog enrichment

Updated: 2026-10-05

This pass enriched the Bengali-alphabetical catalog from item 11 through item 37 and preserved the exact catalog order used by the site. Retailer data were treated as sources, not as the organizing principle.

## Deliberately unresolved / conflict-handled fields

- **ইসলামের শাস্তি আইন**: publisher is consistently verified as Bangladesh Islamic Centre, but online sources point to multiple publication years/editions (including academic citations to 2001 and 2015). Edition, ISBN and page count are therefore left blank rather than mixing editions. Unauthorized PDF mirrors were not linked.
- **গণতন্ত্র: ইসলামী দৃষ্টিকোণ**: current Wafilife listing gives a 2026, 152-page second edition, while older sources give a 2019, ~79-page third edition. The site now uses the current 2026 retail edition. The ISBN shown by older retailers is malformed (`9879849435716`) and is deliberately omitted.
- **উসূলুল ঈমান — ৩য় খণ্ড**: multiple retailers verify 368 pages and a 2024 first edition, but one retailer repeats Volume 2’s ISBN. The ISBN is therefore omitted pending a primary-source check.
- **যুবদাতুল বায়ান — দ্বিতীয় খণ্ড**: multiple retailers repeat Volume 1’s ISBN. Edition/year/pages are retained, but the ISBN is omitted pending publisher confirmation.
- **তাযকিয়াতুন নাফস**: ISBN `9848430290` is independently confirmed by multiple title-specific listings. The same ISBN had previously been attached to *ইসলামী রাষ্ট্রে অমুসলিম নাগরিকের অধিকার ও মর্যাদা*; that earlier catalog entry was corrected by removing the unverified ISBN.
- **জঙ্গিবাদের উত্থান বনাম ইসলামের শাশ্বত শিক্ষা**: title/authorship is independently listed online, but bibliographic metadata remain sparse. Existing publisher information was retained; no ISBN/year/pages were invented.
- **সার্বভৌমত্ব: ইসলামী দৃষ্টিকোণ**: publisher and hardcover format are verified; year/pages/ISBN remain blank because no sufficiently reliable title-specific source was found in this pass.

## Rights handling

No third-party PDF download was added merely because a scan was found online. PDF/audio fields remain null unless a rights-safe source is available.
- **Zubdat al-Bayan / Surah al-Fatihah duplicate-era listings**: Rokomari separately lists the 2019 181-page *যুবদাতুল বায়ান ফী ঈদাহি উম্মিল কুরআন* and the 2020 256-page numbered *প্রথম খণ্ড*, while both listings report ISBN `9789849207429`. They are preserved as separate catalog records because the existing site catalog treats them separately, but this should eventually be confirmed with the author/publisher as an edition-vs-distinct-record question.
